Buying a home in Alberta

Alberta has no land transfer tax. That is the good news. What you pay instead are two small Land Titles registration fees — about a tenth of what a transfer tax costs in BC or Ontario.

What you pay instead: Land Titles fees

Both fees use the same formula, in force since October 20, 2024: $50 plus $5 for every $5,000 (or part of $5,000) of value.

  • Transfer of land: $50 plus $5 per $5,000 of the purchase price.
  • Mortgage registration: $50 plus $5 per $5,000 of the mortgage amount.

Compare that with the same $500,000 home in Ontario, where the land transfer tax alone is $6,475. The Alberta fees are real money, but they barely move the total cost of buying.

No first-time buyer rebate — because there is nothing to rebate

Alberta has no first-time buyer relief on these fees; first-time and repeat buyers pay the same. First-time help in Alberta comes from federal programs instead: the First Home Savings Account (FHSA), the Home Buyers' Plan (RRSP), and the GST rebate on new homes.
