Buying a home in Manitoba

Manitoba charges a land transfer tax that tops out at 2% — and unlike BC, Ontario and PEI, first-time buyers get no break on it. Everyone pays the same tax at the same price.

Manitoba land transfer tax rates

Each rate applies only to the part of the price inside that bracket, not to the whole price.

Part of the priceRate
First $30,0000%
$30,001 to $90,0000.5%
$90,001 to $150,0001%
$150,001 to $200,0001.5%
Over $200,0002%

On a $300,000 home the tax is $3,650. On a $400,000 home it is $5,650, and on a $500,000 home $7,650. Because the brackets top out at $200,000, almost every home pays 2% on most of its price.

No first-time buyer relief

Manitoba offers no exemption or refund on its land transfer tax. A first-time buyer pays the full amount, just like a repeat buyer. The help for first-time buyers comes from federal programs instead: the First Home Savings Account (FHSA), the Home Buyers' Plan (RRSP), and the GST rebate on new homes. Manitoba Housing's Affordable Homes Program covers the land transfer tax and fees on the homes it sells, but only in selected rural communities — it does not cover Winnipeg.

More things to know

  • The tax is charged on the fair market value at registration — usually the price you paid.
  • Winnipeg property tax works differently from most cities. It is charged in mills on 45% of the assessed value, so the calculator's $3,600-a-year default may be low for a Winnipeg home — check the tax bill on the listing.
  • Budget about $70 more for Land Titles registration on top of the tax. Your lawyer confirms the exact amount.
