Buying a home in the Northwest Territories

The Northwest Territories has no land transfer tax. The Land Titles Office charges a registration fee scaled to the value — $2 for every $1,000 of the price — plus a fee to register the mortgage.

Land Titles fees

The transfer fee is $2 per $1,000 of value up to $1,000,000 (minimum $100), then $2,000 plus $1.50 per $1,000 above $1,000,000. Registering the mortgage is $1.50 per $1,000 of the amount secured (minimum $80).

No first-time buyer relief

There is no first-time buyer relief on these fees, because there is no tax to rebate. The help for first-time buyers comes from federal programs: the First Home Savings Account (FHSA), the Home Buyers' Plan (RRSP), and the GST rebate on new homes.

More things to know

  • The transfer fee went up in September 2025, from $1.50 to $2 per $1,000 — older pages still quote $1.50, and they are out of date.
  • Most NWT buyers buy in Yellowknife. The same federal mortgage rules apply there as everywhere in Canada.
  • The mortgage fee is charged on the amount secured, including any CMHC premium added to the mortgage.
