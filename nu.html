Buying a home in Nunavut

Nunavut has no land transfer tax. The Legal Registries Office charges a registration fee scaled to the value — $1.50 for every $1,000 — plus a fee to register the mortgage. It is the cheapest territory in Canada to close a purchase.

Land Titles fees

The transfer fee is $1.50 per $1,000 of value up to $1,000,000 (minimum $60), then $1,500 plus $1 per $1,000 above $1,000,000. Registering the mortgage is $1 per $1,000 of the amount secured (minimum $40).

No first-time buyer relief

There is no first-time buyer relief on these fees, because there is no tax to rebate. Nunavut Housing Corporation runs homeownership and down payment assistance programs instead.

More things to know

  • Most land in Nunavut is Crown land, so private purchases are concentrated in Iqaluit. Your lawyer will confirm what tenure the home sits on.
  • The mortgage fee is charged on the amount secured, including any CMHC premium added to the mortgage.
  • There is no territorial sales tax in Nunavut — only the 5% federal GST, which applies to new construction.
