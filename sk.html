Buying a home in Saskatchewan

Saskatchewan has no land transfer tax. What you pay instead are Information Services Corporation (ISC) registration fees — 0.4% of the value to transfer the title, plus a flat fee to register the mortgage.

What you pay instead: ISC registration fees

The title transfer fee depends on the value of the title. The mortgage fee is a flat amount that depends on the size of the mortgage.

On a $400,000 home with a $320,000 mortgage, the fees are $1,600 to transfer the title plus $275 to register the mortgage — $1,875 in total. These rates took effect April 15, 2026; the transfer fee used to be 0.3%, so older calculators still quote the old rate.

First-time buyers and the CMHC premium

There is no first-time buyer rebate on these fees, because there is no tax to rebate. Saskatchewan's First-Time Homebuyers' Tax Credit is claimed on your income tax return, not at closing. One cost that does catch buyers out: Saskatchewan charges 6% PST on the CMHC premium, and it must be paid in cash at closing — it cannot be added to the mortgage.

More things to know

  • The 0.4% transfer fee is new. It rose from 0.3% on April 15, 2026 — if a calculator quotes 0.3%, it is out of date.
  • The mortgage fee is flat, not a percentage. A $400,000 mortgage and a $499,000 mortgage cost the same $275 to register.
  • Only Ontario, Quebec and Saskatchewan tax the CMHC premium. In every other province the premium itself is the only cost.

Figures verified November 2026. Tax rules change — confirm them with your lawyer or notary before closing. This page is educational only, not financial advice.
