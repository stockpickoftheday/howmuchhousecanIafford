About this site

This site answers one question in plain English: how much house can I afford in Canada?

Buying a home is the biggest purchase most Canadians ever make, and the numbers around it — stress tests, GDS and TDS ratios, CMHC insurance, land transfer tax — are usually explained in banker language. This site explains them the way you would explain them to a friend, and it does the maths for you.

The calculator uses the same rules a lender uses: the mortgage stress test (qualifying at your rate plus 2%, or 5.25%, whichever is higher), the 39% gross debt service limit and the 44% total debt service limit, the minimum down payment rules, and CMHC insurance premiums. It also works out your land transfer tax and cash to close for every province and territory, including first-time buyer relief where it applies.

The site is run by Cory Lewis in Vancouver, BC. It is educational only — it is not financial advice, and it is not a lender. Before you make an offer on a home, confirm the numbers with a mortgage broker or your bank, and the tax figures with your lawyer.

Everything on this site runs in your browser. There is no account to create and no sign-up. The numbers you type into the calculator are never sent anywhere.

Figures on this site were verified in November 2026. Tax rates, CMHC premiums and lending rules change, so the numbers are checked and updated when they do.
